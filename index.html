Letter to Savanth

Dear Savanth,

If you're reading this, I hope you’ve found the version of life we once only dreamed of.

Right now, I feel stuck. Some days, I see myself as a failure — not because I’m lazy or lost, but because I haven’t yet become the man I know I can be. I have a job, but it doesn’t bring me joy or pride. It barely supports the family I love, and deep inside, I know I’m meant for more than this daily cycle.

But this isn’t the end. It’s just the beginning.

I dream of a life where I’m financially free — not just to escape stress, but to explore the world, ride to unknown places, try new things, feel the thrill of adventure sports, and learn skills like martial arts that sharpen both my body and mind. I want to live with peace, not noise. One day, I hope we have land to farm, a family we’re present with, and days filled with purpose, not pressure.

I know what holds me back: wasted time, mindless scrolling, skipping the gym, and worst of all — regret. But I also know what fuels me: the wind during a solo ride, the sky from the top of a hill, a small moment when code finally works, a laugh with friends, or the silence that reminds me I’m alive.

From this day forward, I promise you:

  • I will protect my time and attention.
  • I will keep learning — not for the resume, but for freedom.
  • I will show up to the gym, even when motivation fades.
  • I will trade scrolling for doing.
  • I will forgive myself on the bad days — because growth isn’t always clean or linear.

You, future me, are not the product of luck. You’re the result of today’s commitment, one small choice at a time.

Keep going. For us.

With hope,
Old Savanth
